I'm a **Senior Principal Applied Scientist** at [Microsoft](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft/) in Vancouver,
where I build multimodal Agents for M365 and lead them end to end, from ideation through model fine-tuning to shipping.
Before M365, I led the design, science and engineering of Security Copilot, including its agentic behavior.

My 13 years of ML/AI research span Large Language Models, Computer Vision, Representation Learning, Time Series,
Reinforcement Learning, and generative models. Before Microsoft, I led a research team at Borealis AI.
I hold a PhD in Machine Learning from [UBC](http://ubc.ca/), where I was a Vanier Scholar of Canada,
and I'm a former Dental Surgeon (no kidding). I enjoy building/hacking no matter what.

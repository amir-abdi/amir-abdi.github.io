<br>
I'm a Vancouverite, Machine Learning Scientist with a PhD from [UBC](http://ubc.ca/), Vanier Scholar of Canada,
former Dental Surgeon (no kidding), and enjoy building/hacking no matter what.
My 13 years of ML/AI research spans Large Language Models, Computer Vision, Representation Learning, Time Series,
Disentanglement, Reinforcement Learning, and Variational/Adversarial/Autoregressive generative models.

These days
- **Senior Principal Applied Scientist** at [Microsoft](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft/), building multimodal Agents for M365 and leading the end-to-end product cycle from ideation to execution, including the model fine-tuning cycles.
- Prior to M365, I led the design, science and engineering of **Security Copilot**, including its agentic behavior.

Previously
- **Senior Applied Scientist, Research Team Lead** at [Borealis AI](https://www.borealisai.com/), leading research on time-series forecasting, models with abstention, and self-supervised learning.
- **Co-founder and CTO** of [Offerland](https://offerland.ca/), where I architected the ML valuation models now used by the top 5 Canadian banks.
- **Sessional Instructor** of Applied Machine Learning at [UBC](http://ubc.ca/).
